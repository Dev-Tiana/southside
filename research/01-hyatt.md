# Holston House Nashville — official-reference research

- **id:** `01-hyatt`
- **name:** Holston House Nashville
- **scope/source group:** Official reference website: https://www.holstonhousenashville.com/
- **officiality:** **Verified hotel identity; likely official social accounts.** Hyatt’s property page links directly to the Facebook and Instagram accounts below, and Visit Music City independently lists the same accounts plus a YouTube handle. The requested reference domain itself returned no extractable page in this research environment, so its own HTML cross-links could not be confirmed.

## Identity and ownership/brand signals

- **Verified:** Hyatt identifies the property as **Holston House Nashville**, part of **The Unbound Collection by Hyatt**, at **118 7th Avenue N, Nashville, TN 37203**, phone **+1 615-392-1234**. Hyatt’s page contains the hotel gallery and direct Facebook/Instagram links. [1]
- **Verified:** Visit Music City independently matches the name, address, phone, and describes the 191-room boutique hotel; its listing links to the same Facebook and Instagram accounts and to `@HolstonHouseNashville` on YouTube. [2]
- **Verified historical ownership/development signal:** HRI Hospitality’s 2018 announcement says HRI Hospitality, LLC and Hyatt unveiled the 191-room property and identifies the James Robertson Hotel building, location, design, and Unbound Collection affiliation. This supports HRI as developer/owner-side hospitality entity at launch, but does **not** prove current ownership or management. [3]

## Likely official accounts (public URLs)

- Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/HolstonHouseNashville/ — **high confidence**. Public page shows “Holston House Nashville,” Hotel category, exact address and phone, `info@holstonhousenashville.com`, and Hyatt website link. [4]
- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/holston_house_nashville/ — **high confidence**. Public profile name is Holston House Nashville, bio says it is a historic boutique hotel “part of @unboundxhyatt,” and location is Nashville, TN. Hyatt links to this exact handle. [1] [5]
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@HolstonHouseNashville — **probable official account; lower confidence than Facebook/Instagram**. Visit Music City lists this exact handle, but the publicly fetched YouTube page exposed only the consent shell and no channel details or video list. [2] [6]
