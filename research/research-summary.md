# Hotel Website Research Summary

## Scope and confidence

This report consolidates the supplied public-web research for **SouthSide Hotel and Suites Abraka**, with **Holston House Nashville** used only as a Hyatt reference for presentation and content structure. It separates observed or independently corroborated evidence from likely-but-unverified account attribution. Public visibility does **not** establish copyright ownership, legal hotel ownership, or permission to reuse media.

**Confidence labels**

- **High / strongest signal:** multiple public sources converge on the same name, location and reservation number, or an authoritative brand source directly links the account.
- **Likely / provisional:** branding and content strongly match, but no platform verification, legal-ownership record or explicit ownership statement was found.
- **Third-party / unknown:** the source identifies or depicts the hotel but is not evidence that the hotel owns the content.

## 5. Permission and legal-use flags

1. **Written permission is required before publishing third-party or platform-sourced media.** Public visibility is not a commercial reuse license.
2. Confirm whether SouthSide owns each image/video or has a transferable web license from the photographer, videographer, creator, agency or listing provider.
3. Clear **music and audio rights** separately for social videos; platform playback licenses may not extend to a hotel website.
4. Clear **model, guest and staff likeness releases**, especially for identifiable people in rooms, events or service footage.
5. Check **logos, artwork, screens, branded products and artwork** visible in interiors for third-party rights.
6. Do not download or reuse UGC from TikTok, Snapchat, Facebook, Google Maps or Wanderboat without the creator/uploader's written authorization.
7. Do not bypass login walls, rate limits, download restrictions, private links or technical protections.
8. Keep a simple asset register: source URL, original file, creator/owner, permission contact, permitted channels, territory, term, credit requirement and approval date.
9. If current hotel approval cannot be obtained, use newly commissioned photography or clearly licensed stock instead of republishing social media assets.
10. No source found a named legal owner or corporate registration. The website should avoid naming a legal owner/operator until the hotel supplies authoritative details.

### Short design takeaway

For the SouthSide build, use a **clear, visual, category-led hospitality structure** inspired by the reference—not a visual copy: a strong exterior/arrival image, separate room cards, a facilities strip for gym/lounge/food/events, and a compact gallery that lets visitors move from arrival to room to experience. Pair every gallery category with a specific booking or enquiry action. Keep claims factual and confirm room/facility specifications before publishing.

Hyatt's terms restrict copying, reproduction and distribution of its site content without permission. Its photos, videos, virtual-tour assets and social media content should therefore remain reference material only. Photographer, creator, music and other rights for social/tour assets may belong to separate rights holders.
