# Abraka destination research

## Scope

This note supports the SouthSide Hotel and Suites destination guide. It does not claim that the hotel operates tours, sells tickets, guarantees access, or knows current opening hours. Every item below remains a public lead that should be rechecked locally before a guest travels.

## Included public leads

### McCarthy Beach, Abraka

A Delta State Government public video result names McCarthy Beach in Abraka as part of the state's natural attractions. The page supports the existence of the attraction name and Abraka context, but does not provide verified opening hours, ticket prices, safety conditions, or a route from SouthSide.

Source: https://www.facebook.com/DSGovernment/videos/delta-state-continues-to-highlight-its-natural-attractions-as-part-of-a-broader/1885024915428204/

### River Ethiope

Academic and public travel references describe River Ethiope as a Delta State natural feature associated with communities around Abraka. The source supports regional context, not a hotel-to-river travel time, access arrangement, water safety, or a specific tour operator.

Source: https://www.worldscholars.org/index.php/jss/article/view/528

### Abraka and Delta State University

Delta State University has a public website and an Abraka campus identity. This is suitable for local context and event-related planning, while directions and event-day transport should be confirmed with the hotel or the university.

Source: https://www.delsu.edu.ng/

### Park Vega Waterpark, Agbor

Public Delta State travel listings reference Park Vega as a water-park attraction. It is presented on the website as a possible day-trip lead, not an Abraka attraction. The page does not publish an estimated journey from SouthSide or a current ticket and operating schedule.

Source: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g3652170-Activities-c42-Delta_State.html

## Excluded or not published
