# Southside Hotel Abraka — Instagram and related profiles

## Officiality

**Likely official: `@southside_hotel` (Instagram).** The public profile names itself “SOUTHSIDE HOTEL AND SUITES ABRAKA,” publishes the reservation number **07055628339**, links/mentions **southsidehotelandsuites.com**, and exposes hotel-branded highlights for Classic Rooms, Food, Photography, Gym, Reception, Events and room categories.[1] The same phone number and Southside branding recur on the hotel website’s indexed pages and on TikTok accounts.[2][3][4] This is strong cross-link and matching-contact evidence, but Instagram does not show a verification badge in the fetched public page; legal ownership of the account is therefore not independently proven.

**Likely official/managed related profiles:**

- TikTok `@southside.hotel.abraka` — profile title “Southside Hotel,” bio “Quality rooms • Gym • Lounge / Abraka / FOR RESERVATIONS 07055628339,” with 1,181 followers and 62.2K likes when inspected.[3] Its room-tour caption says it tours the drive-in, reception, rooms and corridors, and links the SouthSide Hotel and Suites Abraka place listing.[4]
- TikTok `@southside.hotel` — title “SOUTHSIDE HOTEL.” Posts identify Abraka, show room/amenity/gym/kitchen/reception content, and one post explicitly places the gym at “Winners Road, Opp King David’s Hotel, Abraka.”[5] It appears related and likely hotel-managed, but the public page does not expose the same bio phone number; treat as probable rather than proven.
- Hotel website: [southsidehotelandsuites.com](https://www.southsidehotelandsuites.com/) and its indexed room page list “Southside hotel and suites, Winners St, Abraka 330105, Delta State, Nigeria,” phone **+2347055628339**, and `Hotelsouthside@gmail.com`; search indexing also shows social labels `southside_hotel` and `southside.hotel`.[2] Direct fetch from this sandbox failed with connection refused, so these website details are verified from public search indexing, not live page inspection.

**Verified from fetched public pages:** Instagram profile identity, visible bio, reservation phone, follower/following counts at inspection, named highlights and post/reel links; TikTok profile identity/bio/phone and public hotel-tour captions; TikTok’s room, gym, kitchen, reception and hotel-location captions.[1][3][4][5]
