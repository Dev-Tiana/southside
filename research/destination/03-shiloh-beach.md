# Shiloh Beach Resort, Abraka — destination research

**Status: Exclude** for an Abraka-specific SouthSide Hotel destination page.

Shiloh Beach Resort and Apartment is a real, visitor-facing leisure property, but the strongest evidence places it in **Eku, Ethiope East LGA, Delta State**, not Abraka. The resort’s own website names the property “Shiloh Beach Resort And Apartments Eku” and gives the address “River Road, Eku,” more specifically “1, Don2dammy Crescent off River Road, Eku.” It describes a River Ethiope setting and lists sunbathing, swimming, beach volleyball, and boating among its beach activities. [1]

The resort’s first-party Facebook page independently identifies it as “Shiloh Beach Resort And Apartment (Shiloh Beach Eku)” and lists “River Road, Eku, Delta state, Nigeria.” [2] Traveloka places the property at Don2dammy Crescent, River Road, Ethiope East, Delta, Nigeria, and lists a private beach, bar/beach bar, restaurant, parking, and Wi-Fi. [3] Trip.com also lists the address as Don2dammy Crescent, River Road, Eku, Delta State, Nigeria. [4]

A local regional source describes the resort as being in Eku along the Ethiope River and reports swimming, canoeing, boat riding, picnicking, and on-site apartments. These activity claims are useful corroboration, but the source is not the operator or a government tourism authority. [5] Delta State Government has also promoted Shiloh Beach Resort as one of the state’s natural attractions, confirming that it is a recognized tourism property, but the post does not establish an Abraka address or a distance from SouthSide Hotel. [6]

## Location and hotel relevance

The verified location is **River Road / Don2dammy Crescent, Eku, Ethiope East LGA, Delta State, Nigeria**. No reliable source reviewed here establishes that the resort is in Abraka, and no source provides a measured route or distance from SouthSide Hotel and Suites. Do not describe it as “in Abraka,” “near the hotel,” or assign a travel time without separate mapping evidence.

It could be relevant to guests seeking river/beach recreation—especially swimming, boating/canoeing, picnicking, and beach leisure—but its Eku location makes it unsuitable for the requested Abraka destination page as currently framed. It should only be reconsidered for a wider Delta/Eku-area attractions page after the page’s geographic scope and transport relevance are explicitly established.

**Cautions:** operating hours, admission fees, current activity availability, water-safety conditions, and transport/access details were not reliably established. The resort website’s phone link is malformed in the extracted page, so contact details should be verified directly before publication.
