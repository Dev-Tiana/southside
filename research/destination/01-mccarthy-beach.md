# McCarthy Beach, Abraka — destination verification

**Status: verified.** McCarthy Beach is the public-facing name used for Gordon Resort Hotel in Abraka, Delta State. The resort’s own website identifies Gordon Resort Hotel as “popularly known as McCarthy Beach,” places it in Abraka, and describes the beach on the banks of the River Ethiope. [1] The resort’s official Facebook page supplies the more specific published address: **Abraka/Benin Road, after DELSU Campus 3 gate, Abraka, Nigeria**. [2]

An independent Nigerian Heritage destination listing also identifies McCarthy Beach as a riverfront recreational beach in Abraka on the River Ethiope and provides a map pin at **5.82285691668174, 6.108955138939091** (latitude, longitude). [3] This corroborates the destination’s identity and setting; it is not used to calculate distance from SouthSide Hotel.

Visitors are described as being able to swim, take boat rides on the River Ethiope, use a pool, play volleyball, football and paintball, and use the beach bar, barbecue/sausage spot and cocktail venue. [1] [4] A Delta State Government post independently describes swimming, water sports, kayaking and jet skiing. [5] The Nigerian Heritage listing notes marked swimming zones, life jackets for boating/river activities, children’s pools and a playground, but these operational/safety details should be confirmed before publication. [3]

**Hotel-page suitability / guest relevance:** Suitable as a destination-page item for SouthSide Hotel and Suites because the place is independently corroborated, has a stated Abraka location, and offers concrete leisure activities relevant to hotel guests. Present it as a nearby-area attraction or riverfront resort, not as a SouthSide Hotel facility or as a guaranteed public-access beach. No distance or travel time from SouthSide Hotel is stated here because no reliable source reviewed supplied that relationship.

**Cautions:** The resort’s activity and amenity descriptions are promotional. Current opening hours, admission/day-visitor policy, prices, activity availability, water conditions and road access were not independently verified. The Nigerian Heritage listing displays a price of ₦2,000, but it is not treated as current. The official Facebook page and website publish different contact details, so guests should confirm directly before visiting.
