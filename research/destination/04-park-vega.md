# Park Vega Waterpark, Agbor

**Status: verified.** Park Vega Waterpark is an operating waterpark in Agbor, Delta State. Its official website gives the address as **ParkVega Street, Ewuru Road, off Km 68 Benin–Asaba Expressway, Agbor, Delta State**, and links a map pin at approximately **6.2483333, 6.1538889**. The same site lists opening days as **Thursday–Sunday and public holidays, 11:00–18:00**. [1]

Visitors can use waterpark attractions and related amenities. A 2025 *blooloop* feature based on interviews with Park Vega’s founders describes large slides including **Space-Hole, Multi-Surf, Freefall and Black-Hole Wave Combo**, a wave pool, an activity pool for water games, children’s splash areas with shallow pools/tipping buckets/smaller slides, plus a swim-up bar, restaurants, cabanas and picnic areas. [2] Park Vega’s first-party Instagram profile independently confirms the Agbor location and the Thursday–Sunday, 11:00–18:00 schedule; its profile highlights identify pools, adult slides, restaurant, swim-up bar and cabanas. [3]
