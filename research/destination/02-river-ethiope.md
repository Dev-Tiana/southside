# River Ethiope and Abraka River Resort

**Status: verified** for inclusion as a SouthSide Hotel and Suites destination-page item, with operational and safety caveats.

Abraka River Resort is documented in Abraka, Delta State, on/near the Agbor–Sapele Road and on the bank of the River Ethiope. A destination listing provides map coordinates **5.789432, 6.102347** and describes the resort as having a sandy beach, river seating, canoe rental, restaurant/bar, table tennis and beach volleyball.[1] An independent Hotels.ng listing for the related **Abraka River Resort Motel/Rivotel** also places the property in Abraka and describes a sandy river beach, local canoe rides, swimming, table tennis, beach volleyball, bar and restaurant.[2] An unofficial Facebook page for “Abraka River Resort” provides a second map pin in Abraka at **5.788984, 6.111832** and shows recent page activity/check-ins.[3]

The river itself is independently corroborated by a Nigerian government environmental assessment: the Ethiope rises from a spring at Umuaja, flows for more than 100 km to the Benin River, and is used locally for fishing and inter-village transport.[4] The first-party Abraka Turf and Country Club site—an affiliated/nearby Abraka river attraction, not proof that it is the same operator as Abraka River Resort—gives its address as **9 Old Sapele–Agbor Road, Oria, Abraka, Delta State** and describes a scenic walkway to the River Ethiope, bird watching, horseback riding and nature experiences.[5]

For hotel guests, this is a relevant local leisure option: river scenery, a sandy beach, canoe/boat access, swimming reported by listings, picnicking/relaxation, and nature-based activities. It is suitable for a destination page as a nearby Abraka attraction, but the page should describe activities as **reported/available at the resort** rather than guaranteed, and should advise guests to confirm opening, access, fees and water conditions before visiting.

No distance or travel time from SouthSide Hotel and Suites is claimed here because the reviewed sources do not establish that measurement.

## Cautions

- Naming is inconsistent across sources: “Abraka River Resort,” “Abraka River Resort Motel,” and “Rivotel” may refer to the same site or related/renamed operations. Verify the current operator and guest access before publishing contact details.
- The two public map pins differ by roughly 1 km, so use a current map pin or direct resort confirmation rather than treating either coordinate as definitive.
- The government assessment reports faecal-indicator bacteria at some sampled Ethiope surface-water stations; do not promise that swimming is safe. Treat swimming and canoeing as subject to current local conditions and operator guidance.[4]
- The resort activity and amenity descriptions are from a destination guide, a hotel booking listing and an unofficial social page; current hours, prices, equipment and availability are not independently confirmed.
